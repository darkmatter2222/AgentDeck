V3 — INDEXED STREAM DECK MINI MONITOR MOUNT

PRINT FOUR WORKING PARTS
01_Holder.stl: upright wedge cradle and moving hinge section.
02_Reinforced_Wing.stl: adhesive wing, stationary hinge and index plate.
03_Pivot_Pin.stl: 6 mm gravity-retained main pin, 78 mm working length.
04_Locking_Pin.stl: 4 mm gravity-retained angle-lock pin.
No purchased screws, nuts, springs or bearings are required.
Optional checks: 05_Device_Fit_Gauge.stl and 06_Pin_Fit_Coupon.stl.

SETTINGS
Millimetres; 100% scale; PLA; 0.20 mm layers; 5 walls; 6 top/bottom
layers; 40% gyroid infill for holder and wing. Print both pins at
100% infill. Pins are already head-down with chamfered tips at top.
Use a brim on the tall main pin and holder. Print slowly near the
pin tip; cooling matters on a small cross-section.
Holder and wing are exported side-down; use this orientation.
Holder: about 83 x 83 mm footprint, 118.2 mm tall.
Wing: about 69 x 68 mm footprint, 119.1 mm tall.
Both need removable supports for their raised sections.
Use normal supports everywhere, with interface enabled; inspect
slicer preview to ensure hinge barrels and index parts are supported.
Clear support residue from holes. Do not force pins through debris.
The coupon duplicates both horizontal hole sizes. A tuned printer
should produce a hand sliding fit; printer-specific fit is not guaranteed.

DESIGNED CLEARANCES
Main shaft 6.0 mm / bore 6.8 mm: 0.8 mm diametral clearance.
Lock shaft 4.0 mm / holes 4.8 mm: 0.8 mm diametral clearance.
Nominal hinge end gaps 0.7 mm each. Index-arm gap 0.7 mm.
Gravity settles the holder onto the lower bearing/upper index plate.
These are intentionally free fits. Some small movement is expected.

ASSEMBLY
1. Hold the wing upright, tape face toward you and index plate at top.
   Its wing extends to the RIGHT behind the monitor.
2. Put the holder's long middle barrel BETWEEN the wing's lower and
   upper barrels. The holder's top arm goes ABOVE the index plate.
3. Drop the LARGE pin through the top arm and all hinge barrels.
   The large head stays on top. No glue or press fit is needed.
4. Rotate the holder to zero, then drop the SMALL pin through the
   OUTER hole in the top arm and the index plate's zero hole.
5. Seat the Mini from the front, three buttons across and two down.
   The same photo-estimated V2 wedge pocket is retained. Use thin
   internal adhesive for retention. Route cable through the rear slot,
   leaving a slack loop for swivelling.
6. Apply mounting tape to the completely flat monitor-facing wing.
   All reinforcing rails are on the opposite, rear face. Use the
   full practical tape area; keep monitor vents clear. Follow tape
   curing instructions before letting the assembly hang.

ADJUSTMENT
Remove only the SMALL locking pin. Keep the LARGE pivot pin in place.
Turn the holder, align one of its two holes, and reinsert the lock pin.
Outer row: -30, -20, -10, 0, +10, +20, +30 degrees.
Inner row: -25, -15, -5, +5, +15, +25 degrees.
Thus every 5-degree position is available from -30 to +30.
Positive/negative indicate opposite directions, not a preferred side.
The index pin provides a positive stop; no tiny flexible detents.
Do not turn it while locked. Both pins are gravity retained: mount
upright, and remove/support parts before turning the assembly over.
No hard end stops are fitted when the lock pin is removed.

ALIGNMENT AND SIZE
At zero, the nominal front fascia remains at the monitor front plane:
10 mm monitor + assumed 1 mm compressed tape = 11 mm offset.
Turning the vertical hinge changes sideways viewing angle; the face
is coplanar with the screen only at zero. It does not reintroduce the
Mini's desk tilt. Protruding keycaps are not the alignment datum.
Adhesive wing: 76 x 60 x 8 mm. Two rear reinforcing rails add 6 mm.
The hinge needs about 27 mm of space between cradle and tape wing.
The robust index plate extends about 80 mm behind the face plane,
roughly 27 mm farther back than the wedge cradle. Keep this space clear.
Printed angle numerals are recessed; the diagram is easier to read.

VALIDATION / LIMITS
Device housing profile remains estimated from photographs, not scanned
manufacturer CAD. If V2 has not been physically tested, use the device
fit gauge first. Parts have not been physically printed or load-tested.
All six STL files passed closed-mesh and single-component checks.
Holder/wing intersection was empty at all 13 nominal lock angles
(-30 to +30 in 5-degree steps). This is a geometry check, not a load test.
Pins/holes have deliberate printing clearance; zero play is not promised.
PLA and the tape must be kept away from hot monitor exhaust.
